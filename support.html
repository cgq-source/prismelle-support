Prismelle support

Contact

Email abc466254511@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, app version/build, and steps to reproduce the issue. If needed, include a screenshot of the purchase diagnostic report. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or unnecessary personal information.

Playing and saved progress

Place and rotate prisms, mirrors, and lenses to guide light to its targets. Start the tutorial from Home. Game progress, settings, and hint records are stored on your device. Deleting the app may lose them; restoring a purchase does not restore deleted saves.

Pro and Restore Purchases

Open Settings → Unlock Pro (or Pro active), or use the Pro entry at the bottom of Home. Tap Restore Purchases; scroll if needed. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 99 levels, adds 8 shared hints once to the current local balance, and provides 1 exclusive hint per run, used first. Leaving, resetting, or switching levels does not refill it. Replaying a completed level starts a new run.

Price unavailable

Check your connection and tap Retry once on the Pro page. If it still fails, expand Diagnostics and send the displayed details with your support request. Do not delete your app or save to troubleshoot a store connection.

Privacy and license

Read our Privacy Policy. The app uses the Apple Standard End User License Agreement.

Prismelle 技术支持

联系方式

请发送邮件至 abc466254511@gmail.com，说明设备型号、iOS 版本、应用版本/构建号和复现步骤。购买问题可附诊断信息截图。不要发送密码、验证码、银行卡资料或不必要的个人信息。

玩法与存档

摆放和旋转棱镜、反光镜及透镜，让光到达目标。新手教程从首页进入。游戏进度、设置和提示账本保存在本机，删除应用可能丢失；恢复购买不恢复已删除的存档。

Pro 与恢复购买

从“设置 → 解锁 Pro（或 Pro 已开启）”，或首页底部的 Pro 入口进入，点击“恢复购买”，必要时向下滚动。请使用原购买时的 Apple 账号。Pro 为一次性购买，解锁全部 99 关，在现有本地余额上一次性增加 8 次通用提示，并提供每局优先使用的 1 次专属提示。退出、重来或切关不补发；通关后重玩才开启新一局。

价格不可用

检查网络后，在 Pro 页点击一次“重试”。如果仍失败，请展开“诊断信息”并随邮件提供显示的内容。请勿为了排查商店连接而删除应用或存档。

隐私与许可

查看 隐私政策。应用使用 Apple 标准最终用户许可协议。
