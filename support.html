Prismelle support

Version 1.0.2

Version 1.0.2 has 50 main-campaign levels, with the first 19 free. A one-time, non-subscription Pro purchase provides access to the full campaign, with levels unlocked in order. On iPad, Pro also opens the nine-level Solar Journey immediately, without completing the main campaign; completing all nine Solar levels unlocks the four Cosmic Journey challenges. These two chapters are iPad-only and use landscape orientation. New players start with the Astral Lab theme; existing theme preferences are preserved.

The optional Game Center Journey V1 timed challenge remains a separate 99-level challenge, with 9 free levels and Pro required beyond them. Its boards and progress are independent of the 50-level main campaign. A completed result is ranked first by UTC completion date and then by accumulated puzzle time. Game Center sign-in is required to start this challenge; ordinary play does not require it.

Playing and saved progress

Place and rotate prisms, mirrors, and lenses to guide light to its targets. Start the tutorial from Home. Game progress, settings, and hint records are stored on your device. Deleting the app may lose them; restoring a purchase does not restore deleted saves.

Pro and Restore Purchases

Open Settings → Unlock Pro (or Pro active). Before purchase, a Pro entry is also shown at the bottom of Home. Tap Restore Purchases; scroll if needed. Use the Apple Account associated with the original purchase. The first 19 main-campaign levels are free. Pro is a one-time purchase that gives access to the full campaign, currently 50 levels. Levels unlock in order for everyone. Pro adds 8 shared hints once to the current local balance and provides 1 exclusive hint per run, used first. Leaving, resetting, or switching levels does not refill it. Replaying a completed level starts a new run.

Privacy and license

Optional iCloud sync

Active timed challenges and device preferences remain local. Sync merges completed progress and prevents duplicate badge rewards; each earned badge grants one cumulative shared hint, including existing badges once. Old balances may require a one-time selection because earlier versions did not record cross-device spending. Turning sync off does not delete local or iCloud records. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete its iCloud data; you can manage iCloud app storage through Apple’s system settings. Sync depends on your Apple account, connectivity and available storage. Restoring Pro purchases remains separate from restoring an iCloud save.

Prismelle-Support

Version 1.0.2

Version 1.0.2 umfasst 50 Hauptlevel, davon sind die ersten 19 kostenlos. Pro ist ein Einmalkauf ohne Abonnement und ermöglicht die gesamte Kampagne; die Level werden der Reihe nach freigeschaltet. Neue Spieler beginnen mit Astral Lab; vorhandene Themeneinstellungen bleiben erhalten.

Spielfelder und Fortschritt sind von der Hauptkampagne getrennt. Vollständige Ergebnisse werden zuerst nach UTC-Abschlussdatum, am selben Tag nach kürzerer Gesamtlösezeit sortiert. Zum Start ist eine Game-Center-Anmeldung erforderlich; normales Spielen benötigt sie nicht.
