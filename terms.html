User agreement and version 1.0.3 information

Prismelle is licensed under the Apple Standard End User License Agreement linked below. This page explains version 1.0.3 features and purchase benefits; it does not replace that license or limit rights available under applicable law.

Version 1.0.3 has 50 main-campaign levels, with the first 19 free. A one-time, non-subscription Pro purchase provides access to the full campaign, with levels unlocked in order. On iPad, Pro also opens the nine-level Solar Journey immediately, without completing the main campaign; completing all nine Solar levels unlocks the four Cosmic Journey challenges. These two chapters are iPad-only and use landscape orientation. New players start with the Astral Lab theme; existing theme preferences are preserved.

The separate portrait Refraction chapter contains 19 levels on iPhone and iPad: the first 6 are free and levels 7–19 require the same Pro purchase. Pro includes all 50 classic and all 19 Refraction levels, unlocked sequentially within each chapter. Existing eligible Pro owners do not need to buy it again. Chapter progress is independent. Two additional achievements bring the total to seven: Refraction Master requires all 19 Refraction levels; Cosmic Voyager requires all four Cosmic Journey challenges on iPad, in any completion order. Each new achievement grants one shared hint once. Only classic campaign completions count toward the leaderboard; Refraction, Solar, Cosmic and tutorial completions do not add points. Existing completions remain; incompatible saved Refraction placements may reset after a layout update. Language and theme appear first in Settings. Available languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean.

A new save starts with 8 shared hints. Pro adds 8 shared hints once and provides 1 priority hint per run. Leaving, resetting or switching levels does not refill a run hint; replaying a completed level starts a new run. The five classic achievements are First Light, Mirror Whisper, Resonance, Celestial Nexus and Prism Master, earned at main levels 5, 12, 19, 35 and 50. Each badge grants one shared hint once; historical badge rewards and existing ownership records remain protected against duplicate rewards.

The Apple Game Center leaderboard ranks the number of distinct main-campaign levels you have completed, up to 50. Each first completion updates your score when signed in and connected; replaying a completed level adds no extra points. Offline progress stays on your device and syncs when connected. More completed levels rank higher; time is not used. Ordinary play does not require Game Center sign-in.

Purchases, localized prices and refunds are handled through Apple. Restore Purchases restores eligible Pro access, not deleted progress or spent hints. Optional iCloud sync in Settings merges supported progress and hint records in your private Apple iCloud account; historical timed-challenge records and device preferences remain local. Sync requires an available account, connection and storage. Deleting the app can remove local data; turning off sync does not delete cloud data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Más niveles dan una posición mejor; no se compara el tiempo. El juego normal no requiere iniciar sesión en Game Center.

이용 약관 및 버전 1.0.3 안내

Prismelle에는 아래에 연결된 Apple 표준 최종 사용자 사용권 계약이 적용됩니다. 이 페이지는 버전 1.0.3의 기능과 구입 혜택을 설명하며, 해당 계약을 대체하거나 적용 법률에 따른 권리를 제한하지 않습니다.

iPad에서는 클래식을 완료하지 않아도 Pro로 태양 여행 9개 레벨을 바로 시작할 수 있습니다. 모두 완료하면 우주 여행의 네 가지 도전이 열립니다. 두 여행은 iPad 전용 가로 화면입니다. 새 플레이어의 기본 테마는 별빛 실험실이며 기존 테마 설정은 유지합니다. 이전 완료 기록은 보존하고, 배치 변경으로 호환되지 않는 굴절 챕터의 저장된 부품 배치는 초기화될 수 있습니다.
