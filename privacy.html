Privacy policy

1. Scope and developer

This policy describes information handled by Prismelle: Light Puzzles (Prismelle), its developer cgq-source, and this support website. For privacy questions, contact abc466254511@gmail.com. It covers the current iOS app, including Pro purchases and local purchase diagnostics.

The app does not require a developer-operated account. It has no advertising SDK, advertising tracking, third-party analytics SDK, developer-operated gameplay server, developer-operated account-based cloud sync in the current version. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

2. Game data stored on your device

Prismelle stores level progress, completed levels, optic placements, undo history, tutorial progress, and preferences such as language, theme, music, sound, haptics, and rotation speed on your device. It also stores shared-hint balances, per-run hint usage and completion records, and access retained from earlier saves. These records let you continue playing and apply hint rules consistently.

The local hint ledger keeps a one-time Pro bonus flag and the original Apple transaction identifier associated with that bonus, to avoid granting the upgrade bonus repeatedly. The app does not upload this ledger or your game progress to a developer-operated server.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

3. Apple purchases and Pro access

Apple processes purchases, payments, and refunds through the App Store. Prismelle uses StoreKit to request product information and localized prices, verify transactions, check current Pro entitlements, and restore eligible purchases. The app processes transaction identifiers and entitlement status for these purposes; we do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Apple handles information used by its services under its own policies: Apple Privacy Policy / Apple 隐私政策. Restore Purchases restores eligible Pro access; it does not restore deleted game progress, used hints, or the previous local hint balance.

3a. Game Center achievements

Playing does not require signing in to Game Center. When you are signed in, Prismelle uses Apple GameKit to read and report completed achievements. The app stores a game-scoped player identifier and achievement ownership records on your device to associate pending achievements with the correct player. Offline completions remain local until a later synchronization opportunity. Existing unassigned milestones are associated with the first signed-in player; changing accounts does not automatically transfer another player's milestones.

Apple processes achievement and game-activity information through Game Center. Visibility follows your Game Center sharing settings; see Game Center & Privacy. Prismelle does not send this information to a developer-operated server or request access to your friends list. Game Center achievements do not synchronize puzzle layouts, hint balances, or full saved games. Deleting local app data does not itself erase achievements held by Apple.

3b. Journey leaderboard

In versions with the journey leaderboard, starting a timed challenge requires Game Center sign-in. The challenge has separate boards, progress and active-play timing stored on your device and bound to that player. Completing all 99 levels saves a result containing the UTC completion day and accumulated puzzle time. Prismelle submits this encoded result to Apple through GameKit, retaining offline results for later synchronization. Switching accounts does not transfer challenge results.

When you open the global or friends leaderboard, Prismelle reads Game Center player display names, game-specific identifiers, ranks and scores to show completion dates and durations. Apple processes and displays these results through Game Center. The app does not upload board layouts or hint balances with the score, and the developer does not operate a leaderboard server. Removing local app data does not automatically delete scores held by Apple.

4. Purchase diagnostics and TestFlight

When a product request fails, the app can display a diagnostic report and write the same technical details to the device’s system log. The report includes the error category or domain/code, product identifier, app bundle identifier, App Store storefront country/region when available, and app version/build. System logs may also include timestamps and system process metadata. The storefront is supplied by StoreKit; it is not GPS location.

The app’s purchase diagnostic report does not include your Apple Account, payment-card details, receipts, transaction identifiers, game-board contents, or arbitrary error-description text. It is not automatically sent to the developer. You may choose to share a screenshot or report when requesting help. Diagnostic text is kept in the running app; system-log retention is managed by the operating system, not by a fixed retention period in Prismelle.

If you use TestFlight or share app diagnostics with Apple, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports, testing information, or feedback according to Apple’s services and your settings. That is separate from a developer-operated analytics service.

5. Support correspondence

If you email abc466254511@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store it. Please send only information needed for your request, and do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or unnecessary personal information.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of correspondence, subject to applicable requirements. We cannot retrieve or erase game data that exists only on your device.

6. Retention, deletion, and backups

Local saves and settings remain until you or the operating system remove them. Deleting the app may remove local progress, settings, and hint records; offloading the app may retain its data. Device or iCloud device backups are controlled by your system settings and may include app data. Prismelle does not operate its own cloud-sync or backup service and does not guarantee recovery of local saves.

Deleting the app does not delete Apple’s purchase records. You manage Apple Account and purchase information through Apple. Restoring an eligible purchase can restore Pro access on a device without restoring its previous gameplay or hint history.

7. This website and external services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising or analytics scripts, external fonts, or developer-set tracking cookies. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security and may process browser/request information to deliver and protect the site. See GitHub Privacy Statement / GitHub 隐私声明. Visiting this website does not upload your game progress.

Links to Apple, GitHub, music credits, or other external websites open services governed by their own privacy policies. Email and platform providers may process information in countries other than your own under their policies.

8. Children

The app has no chat, user-generated content, or developer-operated account registration. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If a child has provided personal information through support, a parent or guardian may contact abc466254511@gmail.com to request review or deletion.

9. Policy changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date, and provide notices or choices where required. Features not currently available, such as ads, are not represented as active data practices in this policy. Privacy questions and requests: abc466254511@gmail.com.

3b. 光旅通关榜

在提供通关榜的版本中，首次开始计时挑战需要登录 Game Center。独立的棋盘、进度及前台解题计时保存在本机，并绑定开始挑战时的玩家。完成全部 99 关后，本机会保存包含 UTC 通关日期和累计解题耗时的成绩，通过 GameKit 编码提交给 Apple；离线成绩保留至后续同步。切换账号不会转移挑战成绩。

打开全球或好友榜时，Prismelle 从 Game Center 读取玩家显示名称、游戏内标识符、名次和分数，以显示通关日期及耗时。Apple 通过 Game Center 处理及展示成绩。成绩上报不包含棋盘布局或提示余额；开发者不运营自有排行榜服务器。删除本地应用数据不会自动删除 Apple 保存的排行榜成绩。
