Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026. The upgraded benefits below accompany the upcoming app update. Availability follows your installed version; the new shape leaderboard also requires Apple's approval. This page does not change the current App Store price. The purchase sheet shows Apple's actual localized price.

The updated Classic campaign has 69 levels and Shape Collection currently has 23. The first 25 Classic levels and first 3 shape levels are free. Without Pro, one completed optional rewarded ad unlocks up to 3 subsequent levels in the selected mode; fewer unlock if fewer remain. Sequential progression still applies. Previously preserved access remains available.

A new save starts with 8 shared hints. Each eligible achievement grants 1 shared hint without Pro, or 2 with Pro, once. An optional rewarded hint ad grants 1 shared hint without Pro, or 4 with Pro. Existing balances are retained. Completed-level replay hints remain free.

Pro is a one-time purchase giving access to the supported levels without rewarded unlocks, with an additional one-time gift of 30 shared hints. Each run has exclusive hints equal to half its original placeable inventory, rounded up, with a minimum of 4. These are used before shared hints and do not accumulate. Restarting an unfinished level restores its exclusive allowance; leaving or reopening it does not. Spent shared hints are not refunded.

While Pro is active, first completion of Classic levels 1–30 gives 1 shared hint, and level 31 onward gives 2. Each shape's first completion gives 2. Replays do not repeat rewards. A new Pro purchase does not retroactively reward levels completed without Pro.

For existing Pro players, verified current purchase entitlement enables a one-time adjustment: top up the original purchase gift to 30, make up eligible historical first-completion rewards, and add the second unit for previously rewarded achievements. Durable reward records prevent repeated grants after restart, purchase restoration or supported cloud merging.

Pro has no forced interstitials or passive ad loading. Only after both exclusive and shared hints are exhausted may a Pro player choose a rewarded hint ad for 4 hints. Without Pro, interstitials are attempted only after the first clear of Classic levels 8, 16 and 24, at most 3 in total; no available ad is skipped without catch-up. Shapes have no interstitials. Rewarded ads are voluntary; an incomplete, failed or unavailable ad gives no reward and consumes no hints. If saving a completed reward fails, retry saving without watching again.

Optional return reminders are local iOS notifications. After at least 6 distinct completed levels and 48 hours without play, one reminder may be scheduled with your notification permission. The device stores recent play time, eligibility and reminder preferences. Returning cancels the pending reminder; another requires new play. You can disable reminders in the app or iOS Settings. No remote push service, contact access or developer notification server is used.

Update checks send the app identifier, storefront country or region and selected app language to Apple's public App Store information service. Apple receives normal request information such as your IP address. Notes use the selected language and matching store version; if unavailable, the app links to the store instead. Results are cached locally for 24 hours per storefront and language, and prompts for the same version have a 7-day cooldown. No game progress, achievement history or hint balance is included. Review-prompt history also remains on the device; Apple controls whether its rating prompt appears.

2. Game data stored on your device

Prismelle stores level progress, completed levels, optic placements, undo history, tutorial progress, and preferences such as language, theme, music, sound, haptics, and rotation speed on your device. It also stores shared-hint balances, per-run hint usage and completion records, and access retained from earlier saves. These records let you continue playing and apply hint rules consistently.

The local hint ledger keeps a one-time Pro bonus flag and the original Apple transaction identifier associated with that bonus, to avoid granting the upgrade bonus repeatedly. The app does not upload this ledger or your game progress to a developer-operated server.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

3. Apple purchases and Pro access

Apple processes purchases, payments, and refunds through the App Store. Prismelle uses StoreKit to request product information and localized prices, verify transactions, check current Pro entitlements, and restore eligible purchases. The app processes transaction identifiers and entitlement status for these purposes; we do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Apple handles information used by its services under its own policies: Apple Privacy Policy / Apple 隐私政策. Restore Purchases restores eligible Pro access; it does not restore deleted game progress, used hints, or the previous local hint balance.

3a. Game Center achievements

Playing does not require signing in to Game Center. When you are signed in, Prismelle uses Apple GameKit to read and report completed achievements. The app stores a game-scoped player identifier and achievement ownership records on your device to associate pending achievements with the correct player. Offline completions remain local until a later synchronization opportunity. Existing unassigned milestones are associated with the first signed-in player; changing accounts does not automatically transfer another player's milestones.

Apple processes achievement and game-activity information through Game Center. Visibility follows your Game Center sharing settings; see Game Center & Privacy. Prismelle does not send this information to a developer-operated server or request access to your friends list. Game Center achievements do not synchronize puzzle layouts, hint balances, or full saved games. Deleting local app data does not itself erase achievements held by Apple.

3b. Game Center

Game Center ranks distinct Classic completions (up to 69) and distinct collected shapes (currently up to 23) separately, with higher counts ranking first. A shape counts only after its level is completed; entering it, unlocking it or replaying it adds no extra score. Equal counts have equal scores; no time or hint-use tie-breaker is added. After sign-in or a later connection opportunity, saved counts are submitted for the active Game Center player. Apple manages aliases, ranks and visibility. Scores contain no board placements, puzzle times or hint balances. Game Center scores do not restore shape boards. The developer operates no leaderboard server; deleting local data does not automatically erase Apple's scores.

3c. Optional iCloud sync

In versions with iCloud sync, you can enable it in Settings. Prismelle then stores a private CloudKit copy of completed levels, board placements and undo history, tutorial completion markers, achievements and their game-scoped player ownership, completed challenge records, and the shared hint ledger in your Apple iCloud account. The ledger includes claimed badge rewards, unique hint-consumption records, and the original Apple transaction identifier used to deduplicate the Pro hint bonus. A locally stored iCloud-scoped identifier prevents this save from being automatically uploaded after an account change. These records are not sent to a developer-operated server.

Optional private CloudKit sync merges supported progress and reward ledgers without duplicating grants; it is separate from restoring Pro purchases and from Game Center scores. Historical timed records, device preferences and return-reminder state remain local. Shape boards and collection progress remain on the device in this update. Older balances may require a one-time choice. Turning sync off does not delete stored cloud data; deleting the app may remove local data and does not necessarily remove iCloud data. Manage cloud storage in Apple's settings. Sync requires an available Apple account, connection and storage.
