English

Updated 22 October 2026. The upgraded benefits below accompany the upcoming app update. Availability follows your installed version; the new shape leaderboard also requires Apple's approval. This page does not change the current App Store price. The purchase sheet shows Apple's actual localized price.

The updated Classic campaign has 69 levels and Shape Collection currently has 23. The first 25 Classic levels and first 3 shape levels are free. Without Pro, one completed optional rewarded ad unlocks up to 3 subsequent levels in the selected mode; fewer unlock if fewer remain. Sequential progression still applies. Previously preserved access remains available.

A new save starts with 8 shared hints. Each eligible achievement grants 1 shared hint without Pro, or 2 with Pro, once. An optional rewarded hint ad grants 1 shared hint without Pro, or 4 with Pro. Existing balances are retained. Completed-level replay hints remain free.

Pro is a one-time purchase giving access to the supported levels without rewarded unlocks, with an additional one-time gift of 30 shared hints. Each run has exclusive hints equal to half its original placeable inventory, rounded up, with a minimum of 4. These are used before shared hints and do not accumulate. Restarting an unfinished level restores its exclusive allowance; leaving or reopening it does not. Spent shared hints are not refunded.

While Pro is active, first completion of Classic levels 1–30 gives 1 shared hint, and level 31 onward gives 2. Each shape's first completion gives 2. Replays do not repeat rewards. A new Pro purchase does not retroactively reward levels completed without Pro.

Pro has no forced interstitials or passive ad loading. Only after both exclusive and shared hints are exhausted may a Pro player choose a rewarded hint ad for 4 hints. Without Pro, interstitials are attempted only after the first clear of Classic levels 8, 16 and 24, at most 3 in total; no available ad is skipped without catch-up. Shapes have no interstitials. Rewarded ads are voluntary; an incomplete, failed or unavailable ad gives no reward and consumes no hints. If saving a completed reward fails, retry saving without watching again.

Optional return reminders are local iOS notifications. After at least 6 distinct completed levels and 48 hours without play, one reminder may be scheduled with your notification permission. The device stores recent play time, eligibility and reminder preferences. Returning cancels the pending reminder; another requires new play. You can disable reminders in the app or iOS Settings. No remote push service, contact access or developer notification server is used.

Apple handles purchases and refunds. Restore Purchases restores eligible Pro access, not deleted progress or spent hints.

Optional private CloudKit sync merges supported progress and reward ledgers without duplicating grants; it is separate from restoring Pro purchases and from Game Center scores. Historical timed records, device preferences and return-reminder state remain local. Shape boards and collection progress remain on the device in this update. Older balances may require a one-time choice. Turning sync off does not delete stored cloud data; deleting the app may remove local data and does not necessarily remove iCloud data. Manage cloud storage in Apple's settings. Sync requires an available Apple account, connection and storage.
